## Core Positioning Hypothesis

> **From customer challenge to working concept.**

WelcomeMat is the umbrella. The examples should feel like one practical approach with different proof points, not three unrelated businesses:

- **WelcomeMat concept experience:** Improve how customers discover, understand, and engage with a business.
- **Swoop:** Missed-call follow-up as a second practical proof point.
- **Custom discovery/prototyping:** Tailored exploration of other customer or operational problems.

The positioning remains a hypothesis until the meeting produces feedback. Avoid promising a fixed product, category, outcome, or implementation scope before discovery.
