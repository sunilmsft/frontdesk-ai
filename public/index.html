Our Story

Honest Food, Local Roots

The Plateau Kitchen started with a simple idea: serve honest food made from ingredients grown within 100 miles. We partner with local farms — Full Circle, Alvarez Farms, Skagit River Ranch — to bring the Pacific Northwest to your plate. Every dish tells a story of the land and the people who tend it.
