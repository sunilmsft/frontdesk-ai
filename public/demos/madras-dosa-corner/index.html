Our story 🪷

It started over coffee.

"Seattle was picture perfect — but a little bit of home was missing."

It all started with a casual conversation over coffee at a local Starbucks in the summer of 2013. The topic? Life in Seattle, and how much we missed a delicious dosa with an awesome sambar.

Seattle had the lush greenery and the magnificent Mt. Rainier — but it didn't have the tastes of home. So we set out to change that. Since 2015, Madras Dosa Corner has been serving the Seattle metro area with authentic, mouth-watering food from the state of Tamil Nadu — varieties of dosas, authentic sambar, fresh chutneys, filter coffee, and so much more.

Quality. Passion. Customer Service. That's what we strive for.

Menu favorites

Everything made fresh. Vegan, vegetarian, and gluten-free options available. South Indian classics done right.

🫓

Ghee Masala Paper Roast

Our signature. Impossibly thin, perfectly crispy dosa brushed with ghee and stuffed with spiced potato masala. Served with sambar and chutneys.

🌶️

Mysore Masala Dosa

Fiery red chutney spread inside a golden dosa, filled with spiced potatoes. Bold, flavorful, and unforgettable.

☁️

Idli Sambar

Pillowy steamed rice cakes served with piping hot sambar and fresh coconut chutney. The classic South Indian breakfast.

🍩

Medu Vada

Crispy-on-the-outside, fluffy-on-the-inside lentil fritters. Perfectly spiced and served with sambar and coconut chutney.

🥘

Chettinad Chicken

Aromatic, spicy chicken curry made with freshly ground Chettinad spices. Served with rice or your choice of bread.

🥥

Rava Dosa

Lacy, crispy semolina crepe with a delicate crunch. Light, flavorful, and served with an array of chutneys and sambar.
