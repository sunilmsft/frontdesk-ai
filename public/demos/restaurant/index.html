Our story 🌮

Three generations. One kitchen.

"Every dish we serve carries a piece of Oaxaca with it."

My abuela started cooking for neighbors in our village outside Oaxaca City in 1978. When our family came to Washington, she brought her recipes — handwritten in a notebook that's now held together with tape and love.

Casa Oaxaca isn't trying to be trendy. We're trying to give you the same food that brought our family together around the table for three generations. Mole that takes two days. Tortillas made fresh every morning. Mezcal that tastes like the land it came from.

— Elena & Rodrigo Mendoza
Owners & Head Cooks
