Our Story

A dream that's been
cooking for years

"From the southern region of the Indian sub-continent, we import the most exotic and flavorful spices to create authentic dishes."

Southern Spice brings the bold, fiery flavors of South India to the Eastside. Every biryani is layered with imported spices, every appetizer is fried to perfection, and every dish is a love letter to the cuisine of Hyderabad, Andhra, and beyond.

Whether you're craving our legendary Chicken 65 Biryani, the crispy Bheemavaram Chicken Pakodi, or the tender Goat Dum Biryani — we promise an authentic dining experience you won't soon forget.
