# WelcomeMat Archetype Review Guide

> Use this while reviewing each demo at `http://localhost:3001/demos/`
> Switch between archetypes using the tabs. Test desktop + mobile views.

---

## How to Review

Open each archetype one at a time. Spend 30-60 seconds on first impression before reading the rubric. Gut reactions matter — SMB owners and their customers will judge in <3 seconds.

---

## Review Rubric (score each 1-5 per archetype)

### 1. Visual Distinctiveness
*Could someone confuse this with another archetype?*
- Do the 5 demos feel like 5 different brands, or variations of one?
- Which axis contributes most to the difference? (font? color? layout? icons?)
- Could two of these be swapped to different verticals and still work?

### 2. SMB Trustworthiness
*Would a real local customer trust this business enough to call/book?*
- Does it feel like a real business or a template?
- Are trust signals present? (badges, credentials, photos, reviews, service areas)
- Would you give this business your phone number?

### 3. WelcomeMat Quality Consistency
*Is the floor-level quality consistent across all 5?*
- Are any demos noticeably weaker or stronger than the others?
- Does the "Powered by WelcomeMat" feel earned on each one?
- Would you show all 5 to a potential WelcomeMat customer?

### 4. Scalability / Maintenance Risk
*Could we build 50 sites with this approach without drowning?*
- How much of each demo is reusable structure vs. one-off CSS?
- If we changed the accent color, would the whole site still work?
- How many hours would it take to adapt this to a new business in the same vertical?

### 5. Vertical Fit
*Does the design match what this type of business actually needs?*
- Does the section ordering make sense for how customers shop this vertical?
- Are there missing sections this vertical needs? (hours, menu, booking, pricing)
- Are there sections that don't belong?

### 6. Too Custom / Gimmicky
*What would we regret building into a system?*
- Flag anything that looks cool but would be painful to maintain at scale
- Flag anything that only works for this specific business, not the vertical
- Flag any CSS tricks that would break with different content lengths

### 7. Configurable Knobs
*What should the business owner (or onboarding flow) be able to control?*
- Which visual choices should be per-business?
- Which should be locked to the archetype/vertical?
- What should never be configurable (to protect quality)?

## After All 5

- Which archetype best proves the DNA system works?
- Which one is closest to "could ship tomorrow"?
- Which one needs the most rework?
- What's the #1 thing all 5 share that should become the base template?
- What's the #1 thing that should stay vertical-specific?
