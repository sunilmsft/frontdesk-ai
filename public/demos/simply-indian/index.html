Our kitchen 🍛

Simply authentic. Simply delicious.

"Food that takes you home — no matter where home is."

Simply Indian brings the warmth of Indian home cooking to Bellevue's Factoria neighborhood. Every dish is prepared with authentic spices, fresh ingredients, and recipes passed down through generations.

Whether it's our aromatic biryanis, creamy butter chicken, or crispy jumbo samosas — we believe great Indian food should be accessible, comforting, and made with love. We also cater for all occasions with our own delivery staff, so your next party can taste like India.
